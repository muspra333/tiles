About LuxeTiles

Crafting beautiful spaces since 1985

Our Story

Founded in 1985, LuxeTiles has been at the forefront of the tile industry for nearly four decades. What started as a small family business has grown into one of the most trusted names in premium tiles and home décor.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to bring the world's finest tiles to discerning homeowners and designers. Today, we continue that mission by sourcing the highest quality materials from renowned manufacturers across the globe.
