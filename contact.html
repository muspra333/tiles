Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. Express delivery is available for urgent orders within 2-3 business days.

Do you offer installation services?

Yes, we have a network of certified installers. We can arrange professional installation for all our tile products.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Absolutely! We offer free samples for up to 5 different tile designs. Samples are typically delivered within 3-5 business days.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused tiles in original packaging. Custom orders may have different return conditions.
